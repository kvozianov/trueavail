# Product Vision

Liquidity Control Tower is a settlement observatory for treasury teams that need to know usable cash, not book cash.

The product promise is: show when liquidity becomes usable, where downside risk appears, why a transfer should be rejected or escalated, and what treasury should do next.

The experience should feel funded, restrained, enterprise-grade, and operational. It should avoid generic SaaS decoration and earn trust through dense evidence, readable hierarchy, deterministic receipts, and calm motion.

## Core Narrative

1. Book balance is not usable liquidity.
2. Settlement timing, cut-offs, holidays, buffers, and rail stress change what can be used.
3. Forecast corridors reveal the p05 breach before a human sees it in static balances.
4. Optimization is constrained by survival floors, concentration, CCRI, and rail feasibility.
5. Governance can reject automation.
6. Contingency actions replace false confidence when the optimizer is infeasible.

## Product Register

This is product UI. Design serves operator trust and execution speed. Familiar controls, dense evidence, and consistent states matter more than surprise.

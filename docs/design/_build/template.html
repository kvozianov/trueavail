STEP 02Engineered features

Calendar, lags of 1 / 2 / 7 days, a 7-day mean and volatility, an EWMA and an anomaly score. Scheduled payments are placed on their settlement date per rail.

STEP 03Three models, three corridors

LightGBM (120 trees), gradient boosting (90 trees) and an EWMA volatility baseline each draw their own P5–P95. On day 1 they agree within €50K: the gap is not a model artefact. EWMA, with no features, widens fastest.

Σ w = 1.00
STEP 04Weighted quantile blend

The three corridors collapse into one: 0.55 × LightGBM + 0.30 × GBDT + 0.15 × EWMA, quantile by quantile. Start balance and scheduled flows are added day by day.

buffer €1.00M
STEP 05 · 06Crosses the buffer → gap day lights up

P5 falls under the €1.00M buffer on Tue 19 May: gap €2.13M. Driver: the €4.1M merchant payout (SEPA) leaves that day; the €3.2M card settlement lands only on 21 May. Eight smaller gaps follow from 25 May.

04 how it works · sticky pipeline, two scroll states (stage 03 lit · stage 06 lit)
Stage 03 / 09 · feature engineering
21 features per account-day, placed on the day money actually settles.
Calendar and holidays, net-flow lags of 1, 2 and 7 days, a 7-day mean and volatility, an EWMA and a MAD anomaly score. Known payments are moved to their effective date for the payment rail: the 18 May card settlement lands on 21 May.
548trainable rows out of 600 · 12 anomalies flagged · 7 known-event rows
Stage 06 / 09 · balance corridor
Start balance + scheduled flows + cumulated daily quantiles, day by day.
Each model's daily P5 / P50 / P95 net flow is blended, cumulated from today's settled balance and shifted by the known payments. The result is a corridor, not a number: the downside is visible before it happens.

Where it is weak — stated in the same type size as the wins
  • At h = 3 the model is worse than the baseline (67,662 vs 52,732).
  • Intervals are too narrow for h = 1–3: coverage 0.67–0.80 against 0.90, so near-term gaps can be missed. From h = 4 coverage is on target.
  • The backtest scores history only; scheduled payments are not replayed.
  • Synthetic data from a known process: these numbers describe the method, not real-world accuracy.
